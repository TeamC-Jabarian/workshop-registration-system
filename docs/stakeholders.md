# Stakeholders

## Project Name
Workshop Registration System

## 1. Students
Students are the main users of the system.

Their responsibilities:
- View available workshops.
- Register for workshops.
- Cancel their registrations.

## 2. Education Administrator
The education administrator manages the workshops.

Their responsibilities:
- Create workshops.
- Set workshop capacity.
- View registered participants.
- Monitor workshop capacity.
- Cancel registrations when necessary.

## 3. Workshop Instructors
Instructors are responsible for conducting workshops.

Their role:
- Provide workshop information.
- Conduct educational workshops.

## 4. Training Center
The training center is the organization
that uses the system to manage workshops
and student registrations.

## Stakeholder Goals
- Make registration easier.
- Prevent duplicate registrations.
- Control workshop capacity.
- Improve workshop management.
- Provide accurate participant reports.
